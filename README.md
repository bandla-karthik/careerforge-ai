# CareerForge AI

## Multi-Agent Career & Interview Intelligence Platform

CareerForge AI is an intelligent career development web application designed to help students and job seekers analyze their resumes, identify skill gaps, optimize resumes for job descriptions, practice interviews, evaluate interview performance, and create personalized career development plans.

The platform brings multiple specialized career-analysis modules together into a unified AI-inspired multi-agent workflow.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Finding the right career path requires more than simply creating a resume.

Students and job seekers often face problems such as:

- Not knowing which skills are missing for a target job
- Difficulty understanding job descriptions
- Resumes that do not match specific job requirements
- Lack of realistic interview practice
- Difficulty evaluating interview performance
- Unclear career progression paths
- No centralized platform for career preparation

**CareerForge AI** addresses these challenges through a unified career intelligence platform.

The system analyzes a user's resume and target job requirements, identifies skill gaps, provides resume optimization suggestions, conducts an interactive interview, evaluates answers, and generates career-oriented recommendations.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Students and job seekers often use separate tools for resume building, job analysis, interview preparation, skill-gap identification, and career planning.

This fragmented approach makes career preparation time-consuming and difficult to manage.

CareerForge AI provides a centralized platform that combines these career preparation activities into one intelligent workflow.

---

# 💡 Proposed Solution

CareerForge AI provides a unified platform containing specialized career intelligence modules.

The system can:

1. Analyze resumes
2. Analyze job descriptions
3. Extract relevant skills
4. Compare candidate skills with job requirements
5. Identify skill gaps
6. Provide resume optimization suggestions
7. Conduct AI-assisted interviews
8. Capture spoken or typed interview answers
9. Evaluate interview responses
10. Generate adaptive follow-up questions
11. Create career development plans
12. Visualize career progress through a Career Digital Twin

# ⭐ Key Features

## 📄 Resume Analyzer

Upload a resume and extract relevant information such as:

- Technical skills
- Programming languages
- Tools and technologies
- Projects
- Education
- Experience
- Certifications

### Supported Resume Formats

- PDF
- DOCX
- TXT

The application uses browser-based document processing technologies such as PDF.js and Mammoth.js.

---

# 💼 Job Description Analyzer

Users can provide a target job description.

CareerForge analyzes the job requirements and identifies:

- Required skills
- Technical requirements
- Relevant technologies
- Expected competencies
- Job-specific keywords

This information is then used for skill-gap and resume analysis.

---
